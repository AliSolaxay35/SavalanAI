# 🏔️ SavalanAI

**Biomedical AI And Medical Imaging**

SavalanAI Is A Biomedical Artificial Intelligence Project Inspired By **Savalan** — A Majestic And Powerful Mountain In The Turkic World, Known For Its Unique Nature, Beautiful Landscapes, And Mountain Lakes.

The Name **Savalan** Represents Strength, Stability, Nature, And The Spirit Of Discovery. It Also Carries A Linguistic And Cultural Inspiration Connected With **Sav** — Sound Or Voice — And **Alan** — A Receiver Or One That Takes In, Giving The Project A Broader Symbolic Meaning Of Receiving, Understanding, And Revealing The Unseen.

## 🔬 About

SavalanAI Focuses On Exploring The Use Of Artificial Intelligence In Biomedical And Medical Imaging Applications.

The Project Is Designed To Remain Flexible And Expand Over Time Across Different Areas Of Biomedical AI, Including Medical Imaging, Computer Vision, Image Analysis, Segmentation, Classification, Detection, Computational Pathology, And Other Related Applications.

## 🧠 Areas Of Exploration

* Medical Imaging
* Biomedical Artificial Intelligence
* Computer Vision
* Medical Image Classification
* Medical Image Segmentation
* Medical Image Detection
* Image Processing And Preprocessing
* Computational Pathology
* Deep Learning
* Neural Networks
* Biomedical Image Analysis

## 🎯 Vision

The Goal Of SavalanAI Is To Explore How Artificial Intelligence Can Help Analyze Biomedical Data And Medical Images, While Building Practical Knowledge Through Real-World Datasets And Machine Learning Projects.

The Project May Expand Into New Medical Domains, Imaging Modalities, Models, And AI Techniques Over Time.

## 🛠️ Technologies

Python • PyTorch • NumPy • Pandas • Matplotlib • OpenCV • Scikit-Learn • Medical Imaging Libraries
